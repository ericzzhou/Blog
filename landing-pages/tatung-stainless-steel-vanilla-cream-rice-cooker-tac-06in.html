Pro Tips for TATUNG Stainless Steel Vanilla Cream Ric

Get the most out of your purchase with these practical tips

🍚

Rinse Rice 3 Times

Always rinse rice until water runs clear. This removes excess starch and prevents gummy texture. Use the included measuring cup for accuracy.

⚡

Use the Right Setting

White rice ≠ brown rice ≠ sushi rice. Each grain type has an optimal cooking program. Using the wrong setting affects texture significantly.

🧹

Clean the Steam Vent

Monthly, check and clean the steam vent and inner lid gasket. Buildup affects cooking performance and can cause odors.

📏

Water Ratio Matters

Follow the water level lines inside the pot. They account for the rice cup measurement (180ml), not US cups (240ml).

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about TATUNG Stainless Steel Vanilla Cream Rice Cooker TAC-06IN 6 Cups

What is the difference between IH and conventional TATUNG rice cookers?
Induction Heating (IH) rice cookers like TATUNG Stainless Steel Vanilla Cream Rice Cooker TAC-06IN 6 Cups use electromagnetic fields to heat the entire inner pot evenly, resulting in more consistent cooking. Conventional models use a heating plate at the bottom. IH models are better for sushi rice, GABA brown rice, and mixed grain settings.
How do I cook GABA brown rice in TATUNG Stainless Steel Vanilla Cream Rice Cooker TAC-06IN 6 Cups?
Select the GABA Brown Rice setting on your TATUNG cooker. This mode soaks rice at a warm temperature (about 104°F/40°C) for several hours before cooking to activate germination, increasing GABA content. Rinse rice first and use the included measuring cup for accurate ratios.
What size TATUNG rice cooker do I need?
A 5.5-cup cooker suits 1-3 people. A 10-cup cooker is better for families of 4-6 or batch cooking. Remember that 'cup' in rice cooker terms is ~180ml (a Japanese rice cup), not a US measuring cup.
