What Is Melon Soda?

A Japanese cafe classic that has endured for over 50 years.

Melon soda (メロンソーダ, meron sōda) is a bright green, musk melon-flavored soft drink that holds a special place in Japanese drink culture. Unlike mass-produced Western sodas, melon soda was born in kissaten — Japan's traditional coffee shops — during the 1970s when cafe culture was flourishing across the country.

The flavor comes from the musk melon (Cucumis melo var. reticulatus), a prized Japanese fruit known for its intricate netted rind, fragrant orange flesh, and delicately sweet taste. Japanese melon soda captures that subtle, floral sweetness — not the candy-blast of artificial melon, but a gentle, aromatic fruitiness that pairs perfectly with effervescent carbonated water.

Its signature vivid green color is instantly recognizable. In Japan, melon soda is more than a beverage — it is a symbol of summer, nostalgia, and the quiet elegance of kissaten culture. You will find it on seasonal menus at cafes, served in tall glasses with a cherry on top, or enjoyed from a can picked up at any convenience store.

Japanese vs Korean Melon Soda

Same melon family, very different personalities.

Japan

Japanese Melon Soda

Origin: 1970s kissaten (coffee shop) culture. The original melon soda.
Flavor: Subtle, delicate musk melon with floral and creamy notes. Lightly sweet.
Style: Cafe-style, often served fresh with ice cream or cherry. Also available in cans.
Color: Soft, pastel green.
Vibe: Nostalgic, refined, summery.

Korea

Korean Fanta Melon

Origin: Korean Coca-Cola exclusive. A modern take on the melon soda concept.
Flavor: Bold, sweet Hami melon with strong carbonation. More intense and sugary.
Style: Bottled/canned commercial soda. Ready to drink, no preparation needed.
Color: Bright, vivid neon green.
Vibe: Fun, energetic, party-ready.

The key difference: Japanese melon soda emphasizes subtlety and the natural elegance of musk melon flavor. Korean Fanta Melon goes bold with intense sweetness and aggressive carbonation. Both are delicious, but they represent different drinking philosophies — the Japanese approach favors nuance, while the Korean version prioritizes instant impact.

Melon soda originated in Japan. The Korean variant is a more recent creation inspired by the original concept. If you want the authentic cafe experience, Japanese melon soda is the way to go. If you want a punchy, crowd-pleasing soda, reach for Korean Fanta Melon.

How to Make Melon Soda at Home

Two ways to enjoy melon soda — quick mix or from-scratch syrup.

🥤 Quick Melon Soda (5 Minutes)

  • 2 tablespoons melon syrup (Torani, Monin, or Hershey's Melon)
  • 8 oz club soda or sparkling water
  • Ice cubes
  • Optional: vanilla ice cream scoop for a float, maraschino cherry for garnish

🍴 Steps

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice cubes.
  2. Pour 2 tablespoons of melon syrup over the ice.
  3. Slowly add club soda, stirring gently to combine.
  4. For a melon soda float, add a scoop of vanilla ice cream on top.
  5. Garnish with a maraschino cherry or a thin melon slice. Serve immediately.

🍈 DIY Melon Syrup (From Scratch)

  • 2 cups ripe cantaloupe or honeydew melon, cubed
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • A drop of green food coloring (optional, for that classic look)

🍴 Steps

  1. Blend the melon cubes until smooth. Strain through a fine mesh sieve to get about 1 cup of melon juice.
  2. In a saucepan, combine melon juice, sugar, and water. Heat over medium until sugar dissolves.
  3. Reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes until slightly thickened.
  4. Remove from heat. Stir in lemon juice and food coloring if using. Let cool completely.
  5. Store in a sealed jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Mix with soda water whenever you want a fresh melon soda.

Tip: The DIY syrup tastes closest to real kissaten-style melon soda because it uses actual melon. Adjust sugar to taste — Japanese melon soda is known for being delicately sweet, not cloying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about melon soda.

What is melon soda?
Melon soda is a Japanese soft drink flavored with musk melon. It originated in 1970s kissaten (traditional coffee shops) and is known for its distinctive bright green color and subtle, fragrant melon taste. It is typically made by combining melon-flavored syrup with carbonated water and ice.
Is melon soda Japanese or Korean?
Melon soda originated in Japan in the 1970s as a kissaten (coffee shop) creation. Korean Fanta Melon is a separate, later product by Korea Coca-Cola with a bolder, sweeter Hami melon flavor. The original melon soda concept is Japanese, though Korean Fanta Melon has become a popular variant. See our Korean Fanta Melon guide for the Korean take.
What does melon soda taste like?
Japanese melon soda has a subtle, fragrant musk melon flavor that is lightly sweet with floral and creamy notes. It is less sugary than Western sodas and has a delicate fruitiness. The taste is often described as a cross between cantaloupe and honeydew with a fizzy, refreshing finish.
Can I make melon soda at home?
Yes. The simplest method is to combine melon syrup (such as Torani or Monin brand) with club soda and ice. For a DIY melon syrup, blend ripe cantaloupe or honeydew with sugar and water, simmer for 10 minutes, strain, and mix with soda water. A scoop of vanilla ice cream turns it into a classic melon soda float.
Where to buy melon soda in the US?
You can buy melon soda at Japanese grocery stores (Mitsuwa, Nijiya, Marukai), Asian online retailers like Yami and Weee!, and on Amazon. Look for brands like Sangaria melon soda, Mitsuya Cider melon flavor, or import shops that carry Korean Fanta Melon as well.
