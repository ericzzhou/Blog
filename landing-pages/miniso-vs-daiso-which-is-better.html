Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before shopping.

Is MINISO or DAISO cheaper?

DAISO is generally cheaper, with most items priced at $1.50-$3. MINISO items range from $3-$15, with blind boxes and collaborations costing more. For basic household items, DAISO wins on price.

Which has better quality: MINISO or DAISO?

MINISO generally has better build quality and more polished designs, especially for electronics accessories and collaborations. DAISO excels in kitchen tools and storage — simple but reliable Japanese engineering.

Does MINISO have blind boxes?

Yes, MINISO is famous for blind box stationery and character collaborations (Sanrio, Disney, San-X). DAISO does not typically offer blind boxes. If you love the surprise element, MINISO is your pick.

Which store is better for dorm room essentials?

Both are great, but for different reasons. DAISO is better for kitchen basics, storage containers, and cleaning supplies. MINISO is better for cute desk accessories, blind box decor, and character-themed items.

Can I buy MINISO and DAISO products online in the US?

Yes. Yami carries both MINISO and DAISO product lines with US shipping. MINISO also has its own US online store. DAISO has limited US physical stores but no dedicated US online shop.
