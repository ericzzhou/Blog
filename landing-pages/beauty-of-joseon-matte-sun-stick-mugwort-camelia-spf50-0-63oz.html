Pro Tips for Beauty of Joseon Matte Sun Stick with Mu

Get the most out of your purchase with these practical tips

Two-Finger Rule

Apply two finger-lengths of sunscreen for full face and neck coverage. Most people under-apply, reducing actual SPF protection.

🔄

Reapply Every 2 Hours

Set a timer when outdoors. Reapply immediately after swimming, sweating, or towel-drying — even with water-resistant formulas.

🧴

Layer Correctly

Apply sunscreen as the LAST step of skincare, BEFORE makeup. Wait 1-2 minutes for it to set before applying primer or foundation.

📅

Check Expiry Dates

Sunscreen loses effectiveness after expiration. Most products are good for 12 months after opening. Write the open date on the bottle.
