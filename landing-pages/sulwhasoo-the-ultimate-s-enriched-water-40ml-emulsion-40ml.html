Pro Tips for Sulwhasoo The Ultimate S Enriched Water

Press, Don't Rub

Korean serums are best applied by gently pressing into skin. Rubbing creates friction and can irritate. Pat until fully absorbed.

Thinnest to Thickest

Apply in order of texture: watery toner → serum/ampoule → essence → moisturizer. This ensures each layer penetrates properly.

🌙

Night Is Prime Time

Skin repair peaks at night. Use active serums (retinol, peptides, vitamin C) in your PM routine for maximum effectiveness.

Less Is More

A pea-sized amount covers the full face. Over-applying serum doesn't increase benefits — it wastes product and can cause pilling.
