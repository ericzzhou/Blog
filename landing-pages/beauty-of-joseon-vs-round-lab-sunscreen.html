Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before choosing.

Which is better for oily skin: Beauty of Joseon or Round Lab?

Round Lab Birch Juice has a lighter, more watery texture that absorbs faster, making it slightly better for oily skin. Beauty of Joseon has a creamier feel that works well for normal to dry skin.

Do both sunscreens leave a white cast?

Neither leaves a noticeable white cast on light to medium skin tones. Both use chemical UV filters that blend invisibly. On deeper skin tones, Beauty of Joseon may show a very slight cast due to its creamier base.

Which sunscreen is more affordable?

Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun is typically $12-13 for 50ml on Yami. Round Lab Birch Juice is around $15-18 for 50ml. Beauty of Joseon offers better value per ml.

Can I use both sunscreens together?

You don't need to. Both provide SPF50+ PA++++ protection on their own. Layering won't increase protection and may cause pilling. Pick the one that matches your skin type and stick with it.

Which sunscreen works better under makeup?

Both work well under makeup if you wait 2 minutes after application. Round Lab's lighter texture pills less with silicone-based foundations. Beauty of Joseon pairs better with water-based foundations.

Ready to Pick Your Sunscreen?

Both are top-tier Korean sunscreens. You can't go wrong with either.
