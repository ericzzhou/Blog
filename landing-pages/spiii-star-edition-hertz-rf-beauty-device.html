Pro Tips for YAMAN SPIII Star Edition Hertz RF Beauty

Get the most out of your purchase with these practical tips

🧴

Use Conductive Gel

RF and microcurrent devices need a conductive medium. Use the recommended gel or a water-based serum — never on dry skin.

🔄

Consistent Motion

Keep the device moving in upward, outward strokes. Staying in one spot too long can cause irritation or uneven results.

📅

3-5 Times Per Week

More is not better. Skin needs recovery time between sessions. Follow the recommended frequency for your device type.

🧹

Clean After Each Use

Wipe the device head with a soft cloth after every session. Buildup affects conductivity and can harbor bacteria.
