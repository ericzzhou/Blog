50+ Years of Ginseng Mastery

Sulwhasoo didn't just join the skincare industry — it pioneered Korean herbal beauty. From a single ginseng berry in 1966 to the world's most awarded K-beauty brand, every product carries decades of research.

1966
The Ginseng Berry Discovery

Suh Sung-hwan, founder of Amorepacific, discovered that ginseng berries — the rarest part of the ginseng plant — contained extraordinary skin-rejuvenating compounds. This led to the creation of Korea's first ginseng-powered skincare product, launching an entirely new category of Korean herbal beauty.

1997
Sulwhasoo Brand Born

Amorepacific officially launches Sulwhasoo as its premier luxury skincare line. The name combines "sul" (snow, representing purity) and "whasoo" (flower water, representing botanical nourishment). It becomes the first Korean brand to gain recognition in the global luxury beauty market.

2008 → Today
Ginseng Science Revolution

Sulwhasoo's R&D center develops Capsuling Technology and discovers ginsenoside compound Rg3 — a proprietary anti-aging molecule found nowhere else in skincare. The brand files over 700 patents related to ginseng extraction and delivery systems, making it the most scientifically advanced K-beauty brand in the world.

Pro Tips for Sulwhasoo The Ultimate S Eye Cream 20ml

Get the most out of your purchase with these practical tips

🤲

Press, Don't Rub

Korean serums are best applied by gently pressing into skin. Rubbing creates friction and can irritate. Pat until fully absorbed.

📐

Thinnest to Thickest

Apply in order of texture: watery toner → serum/ampoule → essence → moisturizer. This ensures each layer penetrates properly.

🌙

Night Is Prime Time

Skin repair peaks at night. Use active serums (retinol, peptides, vitamin C) in your PM routine for maximum effectiveness.

💧

Less Is More

A pea-sized amount covers the full face. Over-applying serum doesn't increase benefits — it wastes product and can cause pilling.
