140+ Years of Japanese Sparkling History

Mitsuya Cider is not just a soda — it is a living piece of Japanese history. Born during the Meiji era when Japan was opening to the world, it became the country's first sparkling soft drink.

1884
The Birth of Japanese Cider

During Japan's Meiji era of rapid modernization and Westernization, Mitsuya Cider is created as one of Japan's first carbonated soft drinks. The name "cider" is adapted from the Western concept but transformed into something uniquely Japanese — a sweet, clear, apple-flavored sparkling drink with no alcohol. It represents Japan's ability to take foreign concepts and make them distinctly its own.

Early 1900s
A National Refreshment

Mitsuya Cider spreads across Japan, becoming a fixture at festivals, restaurants, and family gatherings. It becomes the go-to celebratory drink for those who do not drink alcohol — a toast with Mitsuya Cider becomes a Japanese tradition. The distinctive gold-label design becomes instantly recognizable across the country.

Mid-1900s
Asahi Takes the Helm

Asahi, one of Japan's largest beverage companies, takes over production and modernizes the brand while preserving its classic character. Mitsuya Cider expands its product line with new variations while keeping the original formula intact. The brand becomes part of Asahi's portfolio of iconic Japanese beverages.

Today
A Living Classic

Now over 140 years old, Mitsuya Cider remains a beloved Japanese soft drink. It is available in convenience stores, supermarkets, and vending machines across Japan. International consumers are beginning to discover this piece of Japanese beverage history. The same crisp apple fizz that refreshed Meiji-era Japan continues to delight drinkers worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Mitsuya Cider Japanese Apple Soda

Is Mitsuya Cider alcoholic?
No, Mitsuya Cider is completely non-alcoholic. This is one of the most common points of confusion for Western consumers. In Japan, the word "cider" refers to a type of sweet, carbonated soft drink — not an alcoholic beverage like hard cider in the West. Mitsuya Cider is a family-friendly apple-flavored sparkling soda with 0% alcohol. It has been non-alcoholic since its creation in 1884.
What does Mitsuya Cider taste like?
Mitsuya Cider tastes like a light, crisp apple-flavored sparkling soda. Imagine a cross between Sprite and a very mild apple juice with fine, refreshing carbonation. The apple flavor is present but subtle — not overpowering. It is sweet but not cloying, with a clean finish. The carbonation is lively but gentle, typical of Japanese sodas.
Mitsuya Cider vs Ramune — what is the difference?
Both are classic Japanese carbonated soft drinks, but they differ in flavor and format. Ramune is a lemon-lime flavored soda with iconic marble-sealed bottles. Mitsuya Cider is apple-flavored and comes in standard bottles and cans. Ramune has a more playful opening ritual, while Mitsuya Cider is about straightforward refreshment. Both are non-alcoholic and both have been around since the Meiji era.
