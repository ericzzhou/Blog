What is Laoganma spicy chili crisp?

Laoganma (老干妈 / Lao Gan Ma) spicy chili crisp is a Guizhou-born Chinese condiment: fried chili flakes and aromatics suspended in oil, often with fermented soybeans (豆豉) and Sichuan peppercorn. Chefs and home cooks reach for it when they want chili heat and crunch — the opposite of pourable hot sauce.

In US search results the same product line often appears as “Spicy Chili Crisp” in 7.41 oz jars at Amazon, Walmart, and Target. The 210g jar here is that format for online shoppers who want a compact starter size. For a larger jar on this site, see the 7.4 oz Laoganma page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about LAOGANMA Spicy Crispy Chili Oil 210g

What is Laoganma spicy chili crisp?
It is a crunchy chili-flake condiment in oil (chili crisp), not a thin vinegar-based hot sauce. The classic jar mixes chili, oil, and savory bits such as fermented soybeans — see Lao Gan Ma on Wikipedia for brand history.
How do you use Laoganma spicy chili crisp?
Spoon it onto noodles, dumplings, fried eggs, or leftovers; mix with soy sauce and vinegar for a dumpling dip; or finish stir-fries off heat so the crispy bits stay crunchy. Start with 1 teaspoon — the oil carries a lot of heat.
Is Laoganma spicy chili crisp the best brand?
“Best” depends on what you want. Laoganma is the reference Chinese chili crisp many cooks compare others against (chunky, fermented-soybean savoriness). Japanese-style chili oils (e.g. garlic-forward S&B) taste cleaner and less fermented. If you want the classic Chinese chili-crisp profile, Laoganma is the default starting point.
How long does LAOGANMA Spicy Crispy Chili Oil 210g last after opening?
Once opened, refrigerate and use within 3–6 months for best flavor. Unopened jars keep in a cool, dry place until the best-by date on the package.
Is LAOGANMA Spicy Crispy Chili Oil 210g suitable for vegetarians or vegans?
Always check the label on your jar. This spicy chili crisp style is typically built from chili, oil, and plant ingredients such as fermented soybeans — but formulations can vary by SKU, so verify allergens before assuming vegan.
