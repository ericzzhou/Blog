Pro Tips for Fanta Melon Flavor Soda 11.83 fl oz × 6

Get the most out of your purchase with these practical tips

🧊

Serve Ice-Cold

Melon soda tastes best at 38-42°F (3-6°C). Chill cans for at least 2 hours before serving. The cold temperature enhances the refreshing melon flavor and carbonation bite.

🍈

Pour Over Ice

Fill a tall glass with ice cubes, then pour the soda slowly to preserve carbonation. Add a lime wedge for a citrus twist that complements the sweet melon notes.

🍹

Mix a Melon Soda Float

Scoop vanilla ice cream into a glass, then slowly pour cold melon soda over it. The combination of creamy ice cream and fizzy melon is a Korean-Japanese café classic.

📦

Storage Tips

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Best consumed before the printed expiration date. Once opened, drink within 24 hours for maximum carbonation.
