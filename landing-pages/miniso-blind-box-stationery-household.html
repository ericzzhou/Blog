Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping MINISO in the US.

What is MINISO and where is it from?
MINISO is a fast-growing lifestyle retail brand founded in 2013 by Japanese designer Junya Miike and Chinese entrepreneur Ye Guofu. While the brand has strong Japanese design aesthetics, it is headquartered in Guangzhou, China. MINISO operates over 5,500 stores in more than 100 countries and regions, known for minimalist design, IP collaborations, and affordable prices across lifestyle, stationery, beauty, and household categories.
What are MINISO blind boxes and why are they popular?
MINISO blind boxes are mystery figure collections where you purchase a sealed box containing a random figurine from a themed series. Each series features cute character designs — often collaborations with popular IPs like Disney, Sanrio, We Bare Bears, and original MINISO characters. The thrill of not knowing which figure you'll get drives the collector community. Series rotate frequently, so there's always something new to discover. Prices typically range from $5 to $15 per box.
