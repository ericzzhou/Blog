50+ Years of Vitamin Innovation

Oronamin C isn't just a drink — it's a product of one of Japan's most trusted pharmaceutical companies. From its founding to today, Otsuka has poured decades of scientific rigor into every bottle.

1964
Otsuka Pharmaceutical Founded

Otsuka Pharmaceutical is established in Tokushima, Japan. From its earliest days, the company commits to a philosophy of "New Creations in Healthcare" — combining rigorous pharmaceutical science with everyday wellness products that improve people's lives.

1971
Oronamin C Is Born

Oronamin C hits the market as Japan's first vitamin C-fortified carbonated soft drink. The distinctive brown bottle — designed to protect vitamin C from light degradation — becomes an instant icon. The honey-citrus formula wins over consumers looking for a delicious way to get their daily vitamins.

What does Oronamin C taste like?
Oronamin C has a unique flavor profile that combines sweet honey with bright citrus notes and mild, dense carbonation. The carbonation is lively but gentle, making it crisp without being overpowering. Think of it as a cross between a light lemonade and a vitamin tonic with a satisfying fizzy kick.
Does Oronamin C contain caffeine?
No, Oronamin C is completely caffeine-free. It's a vitamin C soda, not an energy drink. How is Oronamin C different from other vitamin C drinks?
Oronamin C stands out with its unique honey + vitamin C formula and Japanese heritage dating back to 1971. The dark glass bottle acts as a shield, protecting the vitamin C and other nutrients inside from light degradation.
