40+ Years of Hydration Innovation

Pocari Sweat was not created by a beverage company. It was created by a pharmaceutical company that understood dehydration at a molecular level. The story begins with a trip to Mexico.

1964
Otsuka Pharmaceutical Founded

Otsuka Pharmaceutical is established in Tokushima, Japan, with a mission rooted in medical science. The company builds deep expertise in intravenous solutions and pharmaceutical hydration, laying the groundwork for everything that follows.

1980
Pocari Sweat Is Born

Inspired by a business trip to Mexico where an Otsuka employee recovered from dehydration using an IV drip, the team asks: what if we could make an IV you could drink? Pocari Sweat launches as Japan's first electrolyte sports drink, with a formula designed to closely match human body fluids.

1980s – 2000s
Becoming Japan's Essential Drink

Pocari Sweat becomes a household name across Japan. It is the drink parents give sick children, athletes reach for after practice, and commuters grab during humid summers. The iconic blue-and-white label becomes synonymous with hydration itself. The name "Sweat" surprises foreigners, but in Japan, it simply means "replaces what you sweat out."

Today
A Global Hydration Standard

Now sold across Asia and increasingly available worldwide, Pocari Sweat remains true to its original formula. Otsuka Pharmaceutical has grown into one of Japan's largest pharmaceutical companies, but Pocari Sweat stays faithful to its medical roots: science-backed hydration that your body can absorb fast. The powder stick format makes it easier than ever to stay hydrated anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Pocari Sweat Japanese Electrolyte Drink

What does Pocari Sweat taste like?
Pocari Sweat has a light, clean, mildly sweet taste with a subtle grapefruit-citrus note. It is far less sweet than typical American sports drinks like Gatorade. The flavor is delicate and refreshing, designed to be drunk in large quantities without feeling heavy or overly sugary. Many first-time drinkers describe it as a cross between lightly sweetened water and a very mild lemon-lime soda. The name "Sweat" refers to the drink's purpose (replacing lost fluids), not its flavor.
Pocari Sweat vs Gatorade — which is better?
Pocari Sweat and Gatorade serve the same purpose but differ significantly in formulation. Pocari Sweat has less sugar (about 30% less per serving), a cleaner ingredient list, and an electrolyte composition designed to closely match human body fluids for faster absorption. Gatorade tends to be sweeter with more artificial flavors and colors. Pocari Sweat contains no artificial colors or preservatives. For those who prefer a lighter, less sugary hydration drink, Pocari Sweat is the preferred choice.
Is Pocari Sweat good for dehydration?
Yes. Pocari Sweat was specifically developed by Otsuka Pharmaceutical (a medical/pharmaceutical company) to combat dehydration. Its electrolyte balance closely mimics human body fluids, enabling rapid absorption. In Japan, it is widely recommended by doctors for illness recovery, heatstroke prevention, and rehydration after fever or diarrhea. It earned the nickname "drinkable IV" in Japan due to its similarity to intravenous fluid composition.
