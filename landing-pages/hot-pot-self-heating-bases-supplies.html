Hot Pot at Home in 4 Steps

Easier than ordering takeout. More fun than any restaurant.

1

Pick Your Broth

Choose a pre-made base packet — spicy, tomato, mushroom, or clear. Add water to your pot and bring to a boil.

2

Prep Ingredients

Thinly sliced beef, lamb, tofu puffs, enoki mushrooms, napa cabbage, glass noodles. Arrange on plates.

3

Cook & Dip

Cook each ingredient in the boiling broth — meat takes 10-20 seconds, veggies 1-2 minutes. Dip in sesame or chili sauce.

4

Enjoy Together

Hot pot is a communal meal. Eat slowly, cook together, and enjoy the conversation. The broth gets better as it simmers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying and enjoying hot pot in the US.

What is self-heating hot pot and how does it work?
Self-heating hot pot uses a chemical heating pack (calcium oxide + iron powder) that reacts with water to generate heat. No stove or electricity needed. Add cold water to the heating pack, place the food tray on top, seal the lid, and wait 12-15 minutes. Perfect for dorm rooms, camping, offices, or anywhere without a kitchen.

What's the difference between Sichuan hot pot and Japanese shabu-shabu?
Sichuan hot pot uses a spicy, numbing broth loaded with Sichuan peppercorns and chili oil — bold and intense. Japanese shabu-shabu uses a light kombu (kelp) broth with thin-sliced meat and vegetables, dipped in ponzu or sesame sauce. Both are communal dining experiences, but the flavor profiles are completely different.
What do I need for a hot pot night at home?
You need four things: a pot with a heat source (electric pot or portable burner), broth base (pre-made packets make it easy), dipping ingredients (thinly sliced meat, tofu, mushrooms, leafy greens, noodles), and dipping sauces (sesame, soy, chili oil). A dual-sided pot lets you serve two broths at once — great for groups with different spice tolerance.
Are self-heating hot pots safe to use?
Yes, self-heating hot pots are safe when used as directed. The heating pack reaches temperatures around 150°C (300°F), so always place it on a heat-resistant surface. Never add the heating pack to hot water — only cold water. Keep away from children during the heating process. All products sold on Yami meet US food safety standards.
What are the most popular hot pot base flavors?
The most popular flavors are: Sichuan 麻辣 (spicy and numbing with peppercorns), tomato (sweet and tangy, great for beginners), mushroom (earthy and umami-rich, vegetarian-friendly), and clear broth (mild, lets ingredients shine). Many brands also offer dual-flavor packs that combine two broths in one package.
