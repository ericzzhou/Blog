Two Different Philosophies

Understanding why each country makes sunscreen differently.

🇰🇷

Korean Sunscreen

"Sunscreen is skincare"

K-beauty treats sunscreen as the final step of your skincare routine. Products double as moisturizers, brighteners, and anti-aging treatments. The goal: make sunscreen so pleasant you actually want to wear it every day.

  • Rice extract for brightening
  • Probiotics for barrier support
  • Hyaluronic acid for hydration
  • Niacinamide for pore control
  • Lightweight, no-white-cast formulas
🇯🇵

Japanese Sunscreen

"Maximum protection, no compromise"

J-beauty prioritizes UV protection above all. Japanese sunscreens use the most advanced UV filter technology and are rigorously tested for water resistance and durability. The goal: protect your skin in any condition.

  • Advanced UV filter combinations
  • Strong water and sweat resistance
  • Long-lasting protection (80+ min)
  • Rigorous SPF testing standards
  • Lightweight "aqua" textures available

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Asian sunscreens.

What is the main difference between Korean and Japanese sunscreens?

Korean sunscreens focus on skincare benefits — hydration, brightening, anti-aging — with lightweight, cosmetically elegant textures. Japanese sunscreens prioritize maximum UV protection and water resistance, often with a more traditional sunscreen feel. Both use advanced UV filters not available in US products.

Which is better for daily wear: Korean or Japanese sunscreen?

Korean sunscreens are generally better for daily wear. They feel like moisturizer, layer well under makeup, and often include skincare ingredients. Japanese sunscreens are better for outdoor activities, sports, and beach days where water resistance matters.

Do Korean sunscreens have better UV protection than Japanese?

Both offer excellent UV protection with SPF50+ PA++++ ratings. The difference is in the approach: Japanese sunscreens often use more UV filters for broader spectrum coverage, while Korean sunscreens balance protection with cosmetic elegance. For most daily activities, both are equally effective.

Which Asian sunscreen is best for the beach?

Japanese sunscreens like Biore UV Aqua Rich and Anessa Perfect UV are specifically designed for water resistance and outdoor activities. Korean sunscreens like Beauty of Joseon are better for daily urban wear. For beach days, go Japanese.

Can I use Korean and Japanese sunscreens together?

You don't need to. Pick one based on your activity: Korean for daily wear, Japanese for outdoor/beach. If you want both, use Korean sunscreen on regular days and Japanese sunscreen on beach/sport days. Don't layer them — it won't increase protection.
