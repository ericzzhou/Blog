Pro Tips for SUNSHINE Dried Red Dates - Jujubes, 80.2

Get the most out of your purchase with these practical tips

🫙

Airtight Storage

Transfer dried goods to glass jars with tight lids. This prevents moisture absorption and keeps flavors intact for months.

🧊

Freeze for Longevity

Goji berries, jujubes, and similar dried fruits last even longer in the freezer. No need to thaw before adding to soups or teas.

🚿

Rinse Before Use

Quick-rinse dried goods under cold water before cooking. This removes any dust or residue from the drying process.

🍲

Add Late in Cooking

For soups and stews, add dried goods in the last 15-20 minutes. Overcooking breaks them down and loses their texture and nutrients.
