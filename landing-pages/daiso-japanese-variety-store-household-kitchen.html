Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping DAISO in the US.

What is DAISO and why is it so popular?
DAISO is Japan's largest variety store chain, founded in 1977 in Hiroshima. Known as a '100-yen store' (similar to a dollar store), DAISO offers over 70,000 products across household, kitchen, stationery, beauty, and seasonal categories. What sets DAISO apart is the quality — Japanese-designed products that are genuinely useful, well-made, and thoughtfully packaged. With over 6,000 stores worldwide, DAISO has a cult following among people who appreciate smart design at honest prices.
What are the best DAISO products to buy?
DAISO is famous for its kitchen gadgets — silicone spatulas, egg timers, food storage containers, and clever cleaning tools that solve problems you didn't know you had. Other top categories include: storage and organization boxes (stackable, modular, minimalist), stationery (memo pads, file folders, pen cases), bathroom accessories (soap dishes, toothbrush holders, drain catchers), and seasonal items (fans in summer, hand warmers in winter). The kitchen and storage categories are where DAISO really shines.

What makes Japanese household products different?
Japanese household products are designed for small spaces and efficient living. In Japan, apartments are compact, so storage solutions need to be modular, stackable, and space-efficient. DAISO products reflect this philosophy — everything has a purpose, nothing is wasteful, and design prioritizes function over decoration. The result is products that work beautifully in any home, especially if you value organization and minimalism.
