This Privacy Policy describes how zhouzk.com SEO Tools (the “Application”), operated by Eric Zhou (“we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you authorize the Application to access your Google account. The Application is a personal administrative tool used to operate and improve zhouzk.com. It is not a public consumer service and does not serve advertisements.

1. Google User Data We Access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the Application may access the following Google user data through these OAuth scopes:

  • Google Search Console (webmasters): site ownership and search performance data for properties you control (for example, sc-domain:zhouzk.com), including indexed URLs, sitemap status, search analytics, and URL inspection results.
  • Google Indexing API (indexing): ability to notify Google of URL updates for pages on sites you own.
  • Google Analytics (read-only) (analytics.readonly): aggregated traffic and engagement metrics for GA4 properties you authorize.

From your Google account, we may also receive basic profile information such as your name and email address as part of the OAuth sign-in process.

2. How We Use Google User Data

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the Application’s functionality for operating zhouzk.com, including:

  • Monitoring search visibility and indexing status for zhouzk.com
  • Submitting and re-submitting sitemaps to Google Search Console
  • Notifying Google when site content is created or updated (Indexing API)
  • Reviewing aggregated analytics to understand site performance and improve content

We do not use Google user data for advertising, credit decisions, selling to data brokers, creating advertising profiles, or training generalized machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models. We do not use Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI or ML models.

3. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade Google user data. We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties except:

  • Google, as required to call the authorized APIs on your behalf
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal process

We do not share Google user data with third parties for targeted advertising, data brokerage, or any purpose other than providing or improving the Application’s functionality.

4. Storage and Security

OAuth tokens and API credentials are stored locally on the operator’s secured computer in ~/.config/zhouzkcom-seo/ with restrictive file permissions. Data transmitted between the Application and Google uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect Google user data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain Google OAuth tokens and locally cached API responses only as long as needed to operate the Application and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

You may revoke the Application’s access at any time via Google Account → Third-party apps with account access. After revocation, we will delete stored OAuth tokens on the next maintenance cycle or upon request.

To request deletion of data associated with your Google account, contact us at eric.z.zhou@outlook.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Website Visitors (zhouzk.com)

Public pages on zhouzk.com may use standard web analytics (such as Google Analytics 4) to collect aggregated, non-personally-identifying usage data (page views, device type, approximate geography). This is separate from the OAuth Application described above. You can use browser controls or Google’s opt-out tools to limit analytics cookies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Application and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our data practices or Google API usage changes. We will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes to how we use Google user data will be reflected here before continued use of the Application.
