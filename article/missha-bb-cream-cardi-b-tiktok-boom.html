02 · The Spike

Why this one went viral

The usual K-beauty virality cycle is "new ingredient → new product → TikTok hype." The Missha moment is the opposite: a decade-old, drugstore-priced product got a celebrity demo and the market responded in days. That's a sign the demand was already there — the video just told people where to point it.

Why this moment differs from past K-beauty cycles

It also fits the 2026 beauty mood. After two years of "glass skin" and heavy glow, the trend has shifted toward barrier-first skin and "bloom skin" — natural, skin-like finishes over full-coverage looks. A BB cream that blurs without caking is exactly the kind of product that benefits.

03 · Before You Buy

What to know before you buy

Shade check

#23 Natural Beige suits light-to-medium skin with neutral undertones. If you usually buy "natural beige" in Korean cushions, this is the same family. Darker or deeper tones should look for other Missha BB shades.

SPF expectations

SPF42 PA+++ is fine for daily commute use. If you're spending hours outdoors, apply a dedicated sunscreen underneath — BB cream is not a beach sunscreen.

Trend vs. staple

The 816%/329% numbers are TikTok Shop sales and search spikes. If the hype fades, the product still has a decade of Korean skincare reputation behind it — this isn't a novelty buy.

04 · The Fine Print

Is it safe to buy? Watch for unauthorized listings

The viral product is legitimate — but the demand spike has a side effect: unauthorized listings. The Philippines FDA issued Public Health Warning No. 2025-0551 against the purchase and use of an unauthorized Missha M Perfect Cover BB Cream SPF42/PA+++ (#23) being sold as if genuine. A counterfeit tube can't be trusted for shade, ingredients, or SPF claims.

How to buy the real thing

Buy only from authorized channels: Missha's official US store, Amazon's official Missha listings, or established Asian retailers like Yami. No batch code, an oddly cheap price, or a third-party marketplace listing with no seller history — treat it as a red flag.
