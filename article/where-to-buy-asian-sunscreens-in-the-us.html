01 · The Short Answer

Where to buy Asian sunscreens in the U.S.?

Asian sunscreens are Korean and Japanese SPF formulas that use modern UV filters — Tinosorb S, Uvinul A Plus, and Uvinul T 150 — commonly approved in Asia but not for U.S. manufacturing in the same way. The safest way to buy them in the U.S. is a specialty retailer with U.S. warehouse stock: Yami ships from U.S. warehouses in 1–3 business days (transit 1–5 days) with authorized-distributor sourcing, lot and expiry QC, and 30-day returns; SokoGlam and Peach & Lily offer curated selections with 2–7 day U.S. shipping. Amazon is fast but variable — prefer brand-fulfilled listings — and YesStyle has the widest selection but ships from Asia in 2–4+ weeks. The practical rule: choose a retailer that discloses sourcing and keeps inventory in the U.S., and check lot numbers and expiry dates on arrival.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Asian sunscreens safe to use in the U.S.?

Yes — many Asian sunscreens are safe for personal use. If you have concerns about specific ingredients, consult the ingredient list and a dermatologist. Sellers should provide full ingredient panels on product pages.

Will I pay customs or import duties?

If an item ships from a U.S. warehouse, customs duties usually do not apply. International shipments may incur duties depending on value and carrier. Some retailers offer DDP (Delivery Duty Paid) at checkout — choose it if available to avoid surprises.

How do I handle returns for sunscreen?

Most specialty retailers accept returns within 30 days for unopened products. For opened items, policies vary; retain photos of packaging and lot numbers for claims, and contact seller support promptly.
