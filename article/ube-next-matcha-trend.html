Why Ube Is Becoming the Next Matcha

Matcha's rise followed a familiar path: an Asian staple → Instagram/TikTok visual → Starbucks menu → mainstream US grocery. In 2025–2026, ube started running the same playbook.

Eric Zhou Aug 11, 2026 Updated Aug 12, 2026 3 min read

01 · The Ingredient

What is ube?

Ube is purple yam, a Filipino staple with a mild, nutty, vanilla-like sweetness and an unmistakable bright purple color. It is not the same as purple sweet potato — which is starchier and less sweet — though the two are constantly mixed up online. In 2026, ube is being called "the next matcha": Starbucks introduced ube drinks in 2025 (the Iced Ube Coconut Latte and Ube Espresso Martini) and extended the line in its 2026 spring menu with the Ube Matcha Latte and Ube Vanilla Macchiato, and Manila Times covered the comparison in July 2026. The pattern mirrors matcha's rise — chain validation, a color built for short video, and supply slowly catching up. In the U.S., genuine ube products like halaya (jam) and ube powder remain scarce on mainstream Asian e-commerce platforms, so purple sweet potato is the closest widely available substitute today.

"On camera, ube looks like nothing else on a menu."

Why the ingredient travels so well
02 · The Pattern

The matcha playbook

  • Chain validation: Starbucks' 2025 Iced Ube Coconut Latte and Ube Espresso Martini gave the ingredient mainstream US exposure; its 2026 spring lineup (Ube Matcha Latte, Ube Vanilla Macchiato) extended it.
  • Social engine: purple drinks and pastries are built for short video — the same dynamic that pushed matcha (green) and taro (purple-ish) before it.
  • Supply catching up: ube powder, ube halaya (jam), and ube-flavored snacks are moving from Filipino grocery channels into general Asian e-commerce, the same way matcha powder did years ago.
03 · What to Buy Now

What to buy in the US right now

Real ube (ube halaya, Filipino ube powder) is still scarce on mainstream Asian grocery platforms. The closest family that's widely available is purple sweet potato — same color story, similar snack appeal — plus matcha itself if you're already on the trend train.

04 · The Verdict

The bottom line

Ube is following matcha's exact playbook, and the ingredient is one to watch for the next 12–18 months. For now, the practical US takeaway is simple: the trend is real, genuine ube products are still catching up, and purple sweet potato is the closest thing you can buy today.

TL;DR

Watch the trend, don't chase fake ube. Try purple sweet potato powder for the color and flavor story until real ube lands on US shelves.
